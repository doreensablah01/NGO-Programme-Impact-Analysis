# Nkabom Youth Foundation – Programme Impact Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses the Bright Futures Skills Development Programme implemented by Nkabom Youth Foundation. It examines participant demographics, regional participation, training track enrolment, attendance, programme completion and dropout patterns.

The analysis uses Excel, Google Sheets and Google Data Studio to transform programme records into meaningful insights that can support programme monitoring, evaluation and decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Assess participant demographics and regional distribution.
- Examine enrolment across the five training tracks.
- Evaluate programme completion, active participation and dropout rates.
- Identify factors contributing to participant dropout.
- Assess attendance patterns across training tracks.
- Provide evidence-based recommendations to improve programme delivery and participant retention.

## Data Cleaning and Preparation

The initial dataset contained 535 participant records.

The following data preparation activities were undertaken:

- Removed 12 exact duplicate records, leaving 523 records for analysis.
- Standardised gender, regional, training track and programme status entries.
- Addressed missing values by using appropriate labels such as "Unknown".
- Standardised age entries and flagged unusual values for verification.
- Reviewed attendance records and flagged values requiring further validation.

## Key Findings

- **Total participants:** 523.
- **Regions represented:** 6.
- **Female participants:** 290 (55.45%).
- **Male participants:** 220 (42.07%).
- **Unknown gender:** 13 (2.49%).
- **Programme completion:** 264 participants (50.48%).
- **Dropouts:** 125 participants (23.90%).
- **Active participants:** 134 (25.62%).
- **Average attendance:** 14.82 out of 24 sessions.
- **Highest regional participation:** Greater Accra, with 137 participants (26.20%).

### Training Track Enrolment

The programme covered five training tracks:

- Digital Marketing – 138 participants.
- Fashion & Tailoring – 99 participants.
- Catering & Pastry – 96 participants.
- Agribusiness – 96 participants.
- Mobile Phone Repair – 94 participants.

### Important Programme Insights

- Transport cost was the most frequently reported dropout reason, accounting for 36 cases (28.8% of all dropouts).
- Mobile Phone Repair recorded the highest track-specific dropout rate at 30.85%.
- Digital Marketing recorded the highest completion rate at 58.70%.
- Mobile Phone Repair recorded the lowest average attendance at 13.47 out of 24 sessions.

*Note: The 2026-C2 cohort was still ongoing at the time of analysis; its completion figures should therefore not be treated as final.*

## Recommendations

1. **Ease transport barriers:** Investigate transport challenges and consider targeted support and accessible training arrangements.
2. **Strengthen retention in Mobile Phone Repair:** Investigate dropout patterns and introduce early follow-up and learner support.
3. **Improve attendance and engagement:** Monitor attendance and identify participation barriers early.
4. **Strengthen age data validation:** Introduce age-range validation and regular data quality checks.
